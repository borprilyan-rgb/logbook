## Quick Answer

Preliminaries commonly describe project-wide requirements such as site establishment, supervision, and temporary facilities. Their scope and payment basis come from the tender and contract documents.

## Practical QS Notes

- Separate fixed costs from time-dependent costs where the pricing basis requires it.
- Check overlaps with measured work rates.
- Record assumptions about duration, staffing, and site facilities.
