## Quick Answer

Unit rate analysis makes the assumptions behind a price visible: materials, labour, plant, productivity, and the allowances required by the pricing basis.

## Practical QS Notes

| Component | Questions to record |
| --- | --- |
| Material | Quantity, waste basis, delivery scope |
| Labour | Crew, output assumption, working hours |
| Plant | Hire period, utilization, operator inclusion |
| Allowances | Overheads, profit, taxes, risk basis |
