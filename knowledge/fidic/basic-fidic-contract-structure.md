## Quick Answer

Begin by identifying the FIDIC book, edition, amendments, and project-specific contract documents. A clause number on its own is not enough to identify the applicable obligation.

## Working Knowledge

Learning outline: build a document map from the signed contract. Record which conditions are general and which have been amended for the project.

> [!NOTE]
> These FIDIC notes are study outlines. They do not reproduce contract wording or define entitlements.

## Practical QS Notes

- Keep a register of applicable documents and amendments.
- Link measurement, variation, and payment records to the actual contract provisions.
- Add references only after checking the applicable edition.
