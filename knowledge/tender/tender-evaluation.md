## Quick Answer

Tender evaluation assesses bids using the criteria and procedures stated for the procurement. Price comparison is only useful when scope and qualifications are understood.

## Practical QS Notes

- Track compliance, exclusions, assumptions, and arithmetic checks.
- Record the basis of any comparison adjustments.
- Keep the evaluation trail separate from bidder negotiations.
