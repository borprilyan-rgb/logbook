## Quick Answer

A bid bond is a form of tender security. The required form, issuer, amount, validity, and conditions must be read from the tender documents.

## Practical QS Notes

- Check the submission deadline and required wording.
- Allow for issuance costs where appropriate.
- Distinguish tender security from performance security.
