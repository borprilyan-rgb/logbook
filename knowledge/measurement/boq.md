## Quick Answer

A **Bill of Quantities (BOQ)** lists construction work in measured items with descriptions, units, and quantities. Tenderers typically enter rates and amounts to price the work.

## Working Knowledge

A BOQ supports consistent tender comparison and may support interim valuations and change assessment. Its contractual status and whether quantities are remeasurable depend on the contract.

## Practical QS Notes

- Use the specified measurement rules consistently.
- Read descriptions with the drawings, specifications, and pricing preambles.
- Check unit consistency, omissions, duplicates, and arithmetic.
- Separate quantity risk from rate assumptions when reviewing an estimate.

## Example

For 24 m³ of a specified concrete item at an illustrative rate of IDR 1,200,000/m³, the amount is **IDR 28,800,000**. Check which supply, placement, testing, and curing obligations the item includes.
