## Quick Answer

A quantity takeoff is a traceable calculation of work quantities from the available design information and the chosen measurement rules.

## Practical QS Notes

- Record drawing numbers, revisions, units, and assumptions.
- Separate gross quantities, deductions, and net quantities.
- Keep measurement evidence so another person can follow the calculation.
