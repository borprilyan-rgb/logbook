## Quick Answer

Curing maintains suitable moisture and temperature conditions after placement so cement hydration can continue and concrete can develop its intended properties.

## Working Knowledge

Methods include wet coverings, controlled water application, retained formwork, and approved curing compounds. The required duration and method depend on the specification, cement type, environment, and application.

## Practical QS Notes

- Include curing labour, water, protection, and materials in the relevant rate.
- Check compatibility of curing compounds with subsequent finishes.
- Avoid counting curing twice if it is already included in the concrete item.
- Record additional protective measures required by site conditions.

## Example

An exposed slab in hot weather may need prompt moisture protection. Confirm the approved method and allow for coverings, labour, and maintenance in the concrete work estimate.
