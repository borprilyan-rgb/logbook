## Quick Answer

MPa is a stress unit. The notation f'c commonly identifies specified concrete compressive strength. Read the project specification for specimen type, test age, and acceptance criteria.

## Working Knowledge

Keep the strength value, unit, and test basis together when comparing quotations. A unit conversion does not resolve a difference between cube and cylinder tests.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not infer that K-350 and f'c 35 MPa are equivalent. Confirm the design requirement.
