## Quick Answer

Water-cement ratio compares the mass of water with the mass of cement in a mix. Specifications may instead define a water-to-cementitious-materials ratio; check which definition applies.

## Practical QS Notes

- Obtain the approved mix design rather than deriving a mix from strength alone.
- Record admixture and supplementary material requirements separately.
- Leave mix changes to the authorized technical process.

> [!QS]
> Confirm performance requirements and supply scope before accepting a cheaper alternative mix.
