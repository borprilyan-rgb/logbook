## Quick Answer

A backcharge is a proposed recovery of costs from another party. Whether it is permitted, and how it is notified and valued, must be checked against the contract.

## Practical QS Notes

- Identify the contractual basis and required notices.
- Keep labour, material, plant, and causation records.
- Distinguish a proposed deduction from an agreed amount.
