## Quick Answer

A variation is a change to the contracted work dealt with under the contract's change provisions. A variation order commonly documents the instruction or approved change.

## Working Knowledge

Changes may affect quantity, quality, design, sequence, or scope. Authority to instruct, notice requirements, valuation rules, and time implications depend on the contract. A cost submission alone does not establish entitlement.

## Practical QS Notes

- Link every change to its instruction and revised drawing.
- Keep an audit trail of quantities, rates, quotations, and approvals.
- Check whether existing rates apply before proposing new rates.
- Track submitted, assessed, agreed, and paid values separately.
- Coordinate time-related impacts with the project team.

## Example

A revised drawing adds a concrete plinth. Measure the net change, check omissions, identify the applicable rate basis, and document the supporting instruction before submitting the valuation.
