## Quick Answer

LOI often stands for Letter of Intent and LOA for Letter of Acceptance. Their legal effect depends on the wording, surrounding documents, and applicable law, not merely their titles.

## Practical QS Notes

- Identify authorized scope, value limits, and duration.
- Check the basis for payment for any early works.
- Refer questions about legal effect to the project's authorized advisers.
