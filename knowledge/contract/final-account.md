## Quick Answer

The final account reconciles the final value payable for the works under the contract, including agreed adjustments. Final certification, retention release, and settlement follow the contract's procedures.

## Working Knowledge

Start with the original contract value and reconcile applicable changes, remeasurement, provisional sums, and other contractual adjustments. Keep unresolved claims and disputed items clearly identified.

## Practical QS Notes

- Maintain a reconciliation from contract sum to final value.
- Reconcile variation records with certificates and payment records.
- Check supporting measurement and approval evidence.
- Distinguish the final value from the remaining balance to pay.
- Review retention and deductions under the contract.

## Example

An illustrative contract value of IDR 1 billion plus agreed net adjustments of IDR 50 million gives a final value of IDR 1.05 billion. Previous certified amounts and contractual deductions must then be reconciled to establish the outstanding balance.
